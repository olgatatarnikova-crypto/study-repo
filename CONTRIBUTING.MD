# Contributing to Our Project

Thank for your interest in contributing! We're excited to see your ideas.
To ensure a smooth process. please follow these simple guidelines.

## How to contribute
1. **Discuss first:** Before you start writing code, please open an"Issue"
on GitHub to discuss the bug you went to fix or thr feature you want to
add. This helps us avoid duplicated work and make sure your idea aligns
with the project goals.

2. **Create a Branch:** Once your idea is approved, create a new branch from
`main`. Please use a descriptive name, for example:
  * `feature/add-user-authetication`
  * `fix/correct-login-validation`

3. **Make Your Changes:** Write your code, add your documentation, and make
your changes within your new branch.

4. **Commit Your Work:** Please follow our commit message guidlines.
